\section{Introduction}
This document introduces the charging behavior of a resistor-capacitor(RC) network. When a capacitor \textit{C} is charged through a series \textit{R} from a constant supply $V_0$, the capacitor voltage rises according to
 
\[
V(t)=V_0(1-e^{-t/RC})
\]
Where the product \textit{RC} has units of seconds and is called the time constant, $\tau=RC$
